The database

Every series here comes from a public release. We type or extract it from the original, check it against the printed totals, keep the labels consistent from year to year, and store each revision next to the figure it replaced.

Getting the data

Now

In the stories

The series behind each story can be downloaded from the story itself, with the sources and the method.

Now

On request

Need a series from this list, or a cut of it? Ask, and we send it with its sources and every caveat we know about.

Planned

A data service

Subscription access to the full database, with downloads and an API that updates as new releases come out.

The data service is being designed now. If your organisation would use it, tell us which series and how often you need them, because that decides what gets built first: rendell@c-economics.com.
