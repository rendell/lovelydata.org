An island’s boundaries, wound into a shell

Each year is one cross-section: eight indicators measured against a social foundation (inner ring) and an ecological ceiling, drawn as the shell wall (outer ring). Each new year adds a chamber at the front; earlier chambers turn around the shell’s axis, smaller and lower, down to the tip.

How to read it

A wedge stopping short of the inner ring is a shortfall; one breaking through the shell wall is an overshoot and leaves a hole in the wall for that year. Follow the holes down the whorls to see how long the wall has been breached.

Illustrative, made-up data for a small island, to test the form. Built with R; the boundary logic follows ggboundary’s doughnut, the winding its conch.
